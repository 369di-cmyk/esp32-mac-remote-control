LocalUnlock.h is an Objective-C adaptation of the macOS 27 lock-state,
system-target verification and directed keyboard-delivery helpers from:
https://github.com/ts1/BLEUnlock/pull/195
Repository: https://github.com/finemax/BLEUnlock
Fixed commit: c4661c8becc6a1556a4d1e42a130f6ab81c1ddf1
PR contributor: finemax
Files: ScreenLockState.swift, SystemUnlockTarget.swift, UnlockKeyboard.swift,
and the relevant AppDelegate.swift integration.

Local adaptations preserve fixed system-process targeting and fail-closed lock
checks. They add per-pair cancellation/deadline/Caps Lock checks and local
Backspace clearing, and replace proximity-triggered unlock with the user's
existing expiring, authenticated, deduplicated request. Passwords are not sent
to ESP32, a network service, logs, or shortcuts. This is an adapted local build,
not an official BLEUnlock release or a claim of upstream endorsement.
